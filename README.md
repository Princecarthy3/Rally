🎮 Rally

Play. Connect. Compete.

Rally is a social multiplayer game platform built for playing quick, competitive games with friends.

Create a private room, invite your friends with a simple code or link, choose a game, and start playing together from different devices.

🕹️ Games

Rally currently features:

* 🏀 Pocket Basketball — Shoot, score, and compete against your friends.
* 🏓 Neon Ping Pong — Fast-paced multiplayer ping pong.
* ✊ Rock Paper Scissors — The classic showdown with up to four players.
* 🔴 Connect Four — Outsmart your opponent and connect four first.
* 🔢 Number Hunt — Test your speed and accuracy in a race to find the right numbers.
* ❌ Tic-Tac-Toe — A quick strategic classic.
* 🎲 Dice Dash — Roll, race, and take your chances.
* 🧠 Quick Draw Trivia — Answer quickly and see who knows the most.
* 😀 Emoji Decode — Decode the emojis before your friends do.

👥 Play With Friends

Rally is designed around small-group multiplayer.

Create a room, invite 2–4 players, and compete together in quick rounds that are easy to start and fun to replay.

⚡ Quick Games

No long matches or complicated setup.

Pick a game, play a few rounds, see who wins, and rematch instantly.
